Premium Cigars at Lucid Southampton

Located in the heart of Southampton, Lucid is your specialist cigar shop, dedicated to bringing the finest Cuban and premium cigars to the city.
Whether you’re a seasoned aficionado or exploring cigars for the first time, we have a cigar for you. Our carefully curated collection features world‑renowned brands including Cohiba, Montecristo, Romeo y Julieta, Partagás, Punch, Hoyo de Monterrey, H. Upmann, Rafael González, José L. Piedra, Quintero, Guantanamera, Backwoods, La Invicta and more.

Every Havana we stock is EMS (English Market Selection) Standard, guaranteeing authenticity and quality. To preserve perfect condition, all cigars are stored in our humidity‑controlled humidor. Whether you’re looking for a classic Cuban or something new to try, our knowledgeable team is always on hand to offer expert guidance and recommendations.

Alongside Cuban classics, we also provide a wide range of imported and everyday cigars, plus EMS‑branded accessories such as cutters, matches, tubes, ashtrays and humidified pouches. Visit us at 111 East Street, Southampton, or get in touch directly by phone or WhatsApp to find out more and experience the icons of craftsmanship for yourself.
